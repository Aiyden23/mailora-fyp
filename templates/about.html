Platform Overview

OPERATIONAL

Mailora is a cybersecurity intelligence platform designed to assist users in identifying, analyzing, and managing phishing threats within enterprise environments.

The platform focuses on phishing investigation workflows, suspicious URL analysis, intelligence reporting, cybersecurity awareness, and operational monitoring concepts.

Mailora integrates phishing email detection, threat analysis, investigation management, cybersecurity awareness training, and phishing simulation features within a unified web-based environment to support phishing identification, analysis, and user awareness.

The system was developed as a Final Year Project (FYP) for cybersecurity and computer forensic learning purposes, emphasizing operational security concepts, phishing awareness, and enterprise investigation workflows.
